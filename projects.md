# Projects

This page provides concrete, self-directed project guides for producing real ethical-analysis artifacts — from case studies to policy briefs to public-facing writing.

## Write an Ethics Case Study Analysis

- [Sample Case Analysis (Online Ethics Center, University of Virginia)](https://onlineethics.virginia.edu/cases/sample-case-analysis) - A model walkthrough showing how to structure a full ethics case analysis, including how to identify stakeholders, clarify values, consider multiple ethical perspectives, and construct an argument for a position.
- [Using Ethical Concepts to Analyze Case Studies (Rock Ethics Institute, Penn State)](https://sites.psu.edu/ethicsofdatamanagement/unit1/using-ethical-concepts-to-analyze-case-studies) - A step-by-step 12-step framework for analyzing ethical case studies, with worked examples and guidance on applying different ethical frameworks to a single case.
- [Writing a Case Study Analysis (RIT Library Guide)](https://infoguides.rit.edu/ethics/casestudy) - University library guide with structure, approach, and resource links for writing ethics case study analyses.

## Create Your Own Ethics Case Study

- [Write On: Contributing an Ethics Bowl Case Study (A2 Ethics)](https://www.a2ethics.org/write-on-contributing-an-ethics-bowl-case-study/) - A guide for writing original ethics cases suitable for competition or classroom use, covering what makes a good case and how to structure the moral tension.
- [Ethics Bowl Case Analysis Guide and Presentation Planner (APPE Ethics Bowl)](https://www.ethicsbowl.org) - Coach-developed guide for analyzing and presenting Ethics Bowl cases, including a case summary template that can be used to structure your own case writing.

## Write a Public-Facing Ethics Piece

- [Op-Ed Writing: The Basics (The OpEd Project)](https://www.theopedproject.org) - Practical guide from the leading organization training experts to write op-eds, covering structure, voice, and how to translate expertise for a general audience.
- [Guides to Public Philosophy (Public Philosophy Network)](https://www.publicphilosophynetwork.net/guides/) - A collection of short, practical guides covering different modalities of public philosophy work, including podcasting, video, community engagement, and writing for general audiences.

## Produce a Policy Brief or White Paper

- [Policy Briefs (UNC Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.unc.edu/tips-and-tools/policy-briefs/) - University writing center guide covering the structure, audience, and approach for writing effective policy briefs.
- [White Papers (George Mason University Writing Center)](https://writingcenter.gmu.edu) - Writing center guide covering the problem-solution structure, audience considerations, and organization of white papers.

## Conduct an Ethical Impact Assessment

- [AI Ethics Assessment Toolkit (Open Roboethics Institute)](https://openroboethics.org) - A systematic step-by-step toolkit for identifying stakeholders, considering potential ethical challenges, and assessing the ethics risks of real AI projects.

## Design Professional Ethics Frameworks and Training Materials

- [A Framework for Ethical Decision Making (Markkula Center for Applied Ethics, Santa Clara University)](https://www.scu.edu/ethics/ethics-resources/a-framework-for-ethical-decision-making) - The widely-used five-lens framework for analyzing ethical decisions, which can serve as a template for designing your own organizational ethics framework.
- [Giving Voice to Values Curriculum (Mary C. Gentile / Darden School of Business)](https://givingvoicetovaluesthebook.com/curriculum/) - A collection of exercises, short cases, readings, and teaching notes focused on practicing how to act on ethical values, which can be adapted for independent skill-building or used as a model for designing ethics training.
